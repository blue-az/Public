Qwen placement crossover

Session-level repeatability for synthetic prompt-prefill time across three distinct RTX 3090 configurations. These measurements are not answer-quality scores.

Home Local Models Benchmark definitions

The narrow intervals are the result: repeated sessions on this host were highly repeatable for these synthetic prefill cells. They do not rank model quality or establish a causal PCIe effect.

Five-sweep results

Fifteen complete fresh-daemon sweeps (five per lane), 24 measured requests per sweep. Within each cell, two request repetitions were reduced to a sweep median; intervals are 95% Student-t intervals across five sweep medians. Tight intervals indicate within-host repeatability for synthetic prefill, not answer quality or cross-day performance. GPU0/GPU1 does not isolate slot causality; Qwen3.6 dual is a separate model/card-count cohort.
